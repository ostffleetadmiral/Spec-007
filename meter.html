Modus Operandi

The Meter does not suppress technology with force. It prices it out of existence. Its method is a four-stage feedback loop — capital investment demands metered returns; innovations threatening unit-cost are starved; abundance anomalies crash prices below zero; regulators then correct the "oversupply" to protect capital assets. The loop repeats, and the civilization's energy growth stays pinned near 2% regardless of the physics available to it.

It wears legitimacy as camouflage: billing accuracy, market stability, national security review. Each mechanism is individually defensible. The compound effect is a ceiling.

Confirmed Operations

WARDENCLYFFE, 1901. Wireless power lacked a metering interface. Capital withdrew. The tower fell. First documented kill.
MAADI, 1913. Shuman's solar-thermal irrigation plant demonstrated distributed thermodynamic power — 6,000 gallons/minute in the desert. Infrastructure spending went to roads and refineries instead. Starved, not outlawed.
SEC-ORDERS, 1951–present. The Invention Secrecy Act screens civilian energy conversion above ~70–80% efficiency and photovoltaics above ~20%. 6,543 orders in effect at FY2025 close.
EPEX ANOMALY, April 2026. French solar surplus drove day-ahead pricing to −€498/MWh — against a regulatory floor of −€500. Response was not industrial mobilization but CfD redesign to restore scarcity. The Meter defended itself in public, on the record, and called it stabilization.

Assessment

The Meter's single vulnerability is that it has no body. It cannot hold a sealed cartridge, read a ledger of fractions, or sign a covenant. Every defense it fields — secrecy orders, curtailment, negative pricing — concedes the same premise: the physics was never the problem.

SPEC-000 anticipated it before it was named: artificial-scarcity value extraction is a prohibited architecture. The covenant is the countermeasure — a network that admits by signature, not by meter.

Countermeasures (doctrine)

  1. Fixed-capacity access fees over spot metering — kill the unit price.
  2. Industrial sinks for surplus — make abundance load-bearing.
  3. Reform the secrecy screen — efficiency is not a weapon.
  4. Verifiable open dossiers — a claim with a harness cannot be priced into silence.
