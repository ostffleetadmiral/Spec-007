Five Laws of Atma Vidya


The 5 Laws of Atma Vidya (Science of the Spirit)
From Dada J.P. Vaswani's "Many Paths: One Goal"

The Law of the Seed (Karma): As you sow, so shall you reap. Every thought, word, and action bears fruit.  
2. The Law of the Wheel (Reincarnation): The physical body dies, but the soul does not. The soul whirls on the wheel of birth and rebirth until liberation is achieved.  
3. The Law of Assimilation (Nididhyasana): True knowledge is not merely intellectual; spiritual teachings must be absorbed and lived out in daily life.  
4. The Law of Reverence (Shraddha): Recognizing that there is but One Life in all, leading to deep faith, respect, and reverence for every living creature.  
5. The Law of Sacrifice / Self-Sacrifice (Yajna): Offering all your work, ego, and desires to the Divine, turning your entire existence into an act of self-surrender and service.
