SPEC-007 retroactive proposal record

Status: Retroactive Article IV creation record (per the OSTF SPEC Governance Rulebook: proposal document, ethics/security assessment, duplication check). Filed at authority level L2 — provenance/audit data.

1. Problem statement

A thermal-cartridge concept ("Ice Cream Tube"): a sealed consumable that produces cold via a solid endothermic reaction, intended as a swappable cooling/power unit. The engineering question: can a consumer-scale cartridge produce thermodynamically sufficient cooling — and can its claims be reduced to auditable arithmetic?

3. Security & ethics assessment

The hazard register lives in spec-007-research-dossier.md (L2). Summary of assessed risks:

  • Calcium carbide + water → acetylene — flammable gas generation inside a consumer artifact; mitigations evaluated in the L1 harnesses (Novec suppression gate, burn-rate ceilings, envelope geometry).
  • Misuse surface — cartridge as a portable acetylene source; addressed by sealed architecture and by the public-face redaction policy.
  • Regulatory — shipping/storage of carbide classified as dangerous goods; flagged not approved for construction, transport, or deployment in the baseline status.
  • Ethics — no human-subject or dual-use escalation beyond the acetylene surface; the L5 drawer keeps convo inputs classified but grants them zero engineering authority.
