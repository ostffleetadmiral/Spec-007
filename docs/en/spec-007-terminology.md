## Faithfulness rules

1. Numeric values, equations, section order, codenames, jokes, and mission language are preserved in the faithful copies.
2. The faithful copies contain an explicit non-approval notice because the source includes hazardous and unverified claims.
3. Technical corrections, source grading, and rejected claims belong in `spec-007-engineering-revision.en.md` and `spec-007-research-dossier.md`, not in the faithful copies.
4. Traditional Chinese uses `不鏽鋼`, `氧化鈣`, `氫氧化鈣`, `介面`, `電網併網`, and `身分` consistently in the standalone copy.
