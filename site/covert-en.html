Impact/crash:

  1. The crush sensor triggers (>5G).
  2. The diaphragm ruptures.
  3. CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂ (exothermic but solidifying).
  4. The reaction mass solidifies in 3 seconds.
  5. Acetylene generation stops.

Storage: Hermetic seal, nitrogen blanket, shelf life of 5+ years.

Transport: UN 1402 (calcium carbide) compliance.

Easter egg: The Jail is like the Great Wall — it protects by standing firm (城牆).

7. RECYCLING LOOP (RETURN TO GOVERNMENT)

Sludge composition: Ca(OH)₂ (calcium hydroxide) + unreacted CaC₂ + carbon impurities.

Recovery process:

  1. Collection: The consumer returns the depleted blade to a swap station.
  2. Calcination: Heat to 900 °C → CaO + CO₂ (capture CO₂ for industrial use).
  3. Carbothermic reduction: CaO + 3C → CaC₂ + CO (electric arc furnace, solar-powered).
  4. Re-manufacture: Fresh carbide is loaded into a new tube body.

Circular carbon: If biochar is used for reduction, the cycle is carbon-neutral.
