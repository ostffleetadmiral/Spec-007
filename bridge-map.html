The Bridge Map — bidirectional evidence (debrief d10)

Status: L2 evidence layer · Authority: derived from the D-series cluster debrief · Pair: bridge-map-zh.html · Mechanism: tools/bridge-map.mjs → bridge-ledger.json, probe-enforced (BRG-01..09) · Coverage: all 30 populated census roots + the archive corpus

The D-series sweeps carded every cluster root. This map goes further: it records what the sibling projects actually settle — executable audits, per-claim verdict tables, second implementations, and recorded experiment data — and which dossier claims that evidence substantiates. Anchors are mechanical checks over the sibling trees: a file that exists, a marker that resolves, a verdict row that parses, a dataset that holds its schema, a live audit suite that runs green. A sibling that moves degrades to absent, never to error — deferral, not defect.

Evidence directions: → sibling evidence substantiating a dossier claim; ← fleet work the sibling carries; ⇄ a convention both trees enforce.

The honest remainder

Sibling wins with no dossier claim to hang on are recorded, not claimed: fabric-compute verbs over the mesh transports, erasure-coded parity shards, secret-share files, deterministic model runners reproducing real oracles bit-for-bit, the consciousness causal-chain audit, and the archaeology toolchain. The cluster expansion adds more recorded-not-claimed holdings: the claims-lifecycle engine's holdout gating, the codon router's genome evidence, the i-vector lab's four-layer verification pattern, the media lab's bit-parity harnesses, the mound's self-flagging addenda, the agent donor's binding-document contract, the integer render pipeline, the private-git sync stack, and the sealed campaign corpus itself. The reverse gaps are recorded in the drawer edition: bilingual twins, signed canon pairs, probe-enforced invariants, and the published classification boundary remain fleet-only disciplines.

Classification doctrine: sibling verdicts are cited as what they are — settled rows, recorded data, executable audits — and nothing is promoted past its evidence class. The framework-internal sentience instrumentation remains a labeled operational definition, not a consciousness claim.
