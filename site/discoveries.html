What it means

The A-tier primitives compose: every higher paradigm inherits determinism from integer arithmetic. B-tier transport + C-tier governance are the NGO's resilience argument — the network keeps working when infrastructure is denied, which is the reason the cover legend exists. The D-tier stays labeled: the dossier records what is proven, what is interpretation, and what is speculation — and the override audit now enforces the difference mechanically.
