"THE ALL IS MIND; The Universe is Mental." "The Universe is Mental held in the Mind of THE ALL."

"As above, so below; as below, so above."

"Nothing rests; everything moves; everything vibrates." "Mind (as well as metals and elements) may be transmuted, from state to state; degree to degree; condition to condition; pole to pole; vibration to vibration."

6. The Principle of Causality

Physics Equivalency: Apparent randomness is an illusion resulting from low-resolution observation. Determinism and quantum probability operate under unbroken universal laws.
7. The Principle of Gender
"Gender is in everything; everything has its Masculine and Feminine Principles; Gender manifests on all planes."
Physics Equivalency: Creation requires the continuous interaction of active/generative and receptive/structural forces across physical, chemical, and mental dimensions.
