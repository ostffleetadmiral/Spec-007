Standing notes

Local storage is advisory. An attacker with write access to this origin's localStorage can clear the fail-counter or rewrite the record — the same is true of any client credential. The wall is network-side: peers verify covenant signatures and role certificates before channels open, and elevated roles require an issuer present in the persisted fano1.issuers roster seeded at genesis.

Key material. The Ed25519 seed exists plaintext only inside WASM memory during a warm session; at rest it is AES-256-GCM under a PBKDF2-derived key (100k iterations). burn clears the record and session; a reload clears the WASM heap.

Passphrases. Offline crack cost scales with entropy, not iterations — see SEC-22. Minimum length is 8; choose a passphrase, not a password.
