The Divine Nature Within


The Divine Nature Within Man
• The Genesis Premise: In Genesis 3:22, God states, "The man has now become like one of us, knowing good and evil." Throughout history, humanity has searched externally for its divine nature, unaware that it remains embedded deep within.

• Jesus's Inward Focus: Jesus redirected humanity inward rather than toward external structures:

  • "The kingdom of God is within you." (Luke 17:21)

  • "You are the light of the world... let your light shine before others." (Matthew 5:14–16)

• Cross-Tradition & Scientific Parallels:

  • Eastern Philosophy (Atma Vidya): Tat Tvam Asi ("That Thou Art") — the realization that the individual soul (Atman) is inseparable from universal divine consciousness.

  • Modern Science & Cosmology: Physical sciences observe that the elements forming human consciousness were forged in star cores—the macrocosm reflected inside the microcosm.

Five Laws of Atma Vidya


The 5 Laws of Atma Vidya (Science of the Spirit)
From Dada J.P. Vaswani's "Many Paths: One Goal"

1. The Law of the Seed (Law of Karma): "As you sow, so shall you reap." Every thought, word, and action acts as a seed planted in the universe. The consequences are inevitable, returning to the individual either in this life or a future one.

2. The Law of the Wheel (Law of Reincarnation): The physical body dies, but the soul (Atman) does not. Life operates on a cycle or "wheel" of rebirth, where the soul continuously incarnates to resolve outstanding karma and progress spiritually.

3. The Law of Cause and Effect: Intertwined with Karma and Reincarnation, this law dictates that nothing in life happens by chance or accident. Suffering, joy, circumstance, and opportunities are direct reflections of accumulated past actions.

4. The Law of Divine Purpose (Evolution of Consciousness): The ultimate goal of human life is not mere survival or material accumulation, but self-realization. Every experience—whether painful or joyful—serves as a lesson designed to awaken the individual to their true spiritual identity.

5. The Law of Oneness (Unity of the Spirit): The fundamental truth of Atma Vidya is that the same divine light (Atman) resides within all living beings. Recognizing this underlying unity dismantles division, ego, and conflict, leading to universal compassion and love.
